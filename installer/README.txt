MFR-Cowboy - free late-'90s indie guitar rig by Music For Robots
https://musicforrobots.com
Source code: https://github.com/music-for-robots/MFR-Cowboy

INSTALLING (zip version)
  Copy the MFR-Cowboy.vst3 folder into:
      C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
  Then rescan plugins in your DAW (in FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins
  > Find plugins). MFR-Cowboy.exe is a standalone version: pick your
  audio interface under Options, and plug your guitar in.

WHAT IT DOES
  A big, clean, bright 100 W combo with six 10-inch speakers, a spring
  reverb and old tape. The grit comes from a cheap distortion pedal
  stomped on and off, and PULSE can do the stomping for you, in time.

  DISTORTION
          Dist, Tone, Level. ON stomps it in.
          PULSE switches it on and off in time with your DAW's tempo
          (1/4, 1/8 or 1/16 notes). With no tempo to follow (the
          standalone), it runs at 120 BPM. The light shows when it's in.
  AMP     Volume, Treble, Middle, Bass, and a BRIGHT switch (extra top
          at low volume settings). AMP + CAB off = just the distortion,
          for your own amp sim.
  SPRING  Spring reverb amount (0 = dry).
  TAPE    How much the old tape squashes and darkens the sound.
  OUT     Output level. INIT resets every control.

  Knobs: double-click or Alt-click resets to the default.
         Ctrl- or Shift-drag gives fine control.
         Click the value under a knob to type in a number.
